## Overview

Laserfiche Workflows, Business Processes, and Forms enable you to build repeatable patterns of activity that can be completely automated and easily configured.

Event-driven business logic orchestration often requires integrations with diverse line of business systems.

Laserfiche Workflows and Business Processes provide Rules or Activities that enable exchanging data with systems that expose functionality via open APIs standards such as HTTP and REST.

## Examples of pre-configured Rules and Activities for HTTP based API calls.
